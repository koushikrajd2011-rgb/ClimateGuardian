HOW TO PLAY

🎯 How It Works

• Color-based path: Enemies follow the brown path. Towers can't go on the path color.
• Not tiled, not grid: Free placement anywhere off the path color.
• Build anytime: You can place towers even during waves!
• Click towers on map: Click a placed tower to see stats, sell, or upgrade.
• Health loss different: Micro 1, CO₂ 1, Wildfire 2, Dozer 3, Methane 4, Heat Dome 5.
• 20 maps: Unique winding paths. Background changes every 10 levels.
• Eco tips: When you place a tower, you learn a real-world action to help Earth!
• Prologues: Story conversations before each zone, personalised with your name.
